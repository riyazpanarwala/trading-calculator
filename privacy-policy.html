Privacy Policy — Universal Trading Calculator

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Universal Trading Calculator ("the App") is a standalone calculation tool. This policy explains how the App handles data.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All calculations happen locally on your device. There are no accounts, no logins, and no analytics or tracking services embedded in the App.

Device Permissions

The App may use the following device capabilities, solely to provide its core features:

  • Clipboard — to let you copy calculated results as text, only when you tap "Copy Results".
  • Sharing — to let you share a screenshot of your trade setup, only when you tap "Share Setup".

No data reaches our servers because the App does not have any servers. Content you choose to share or copy is handled entirely by your device's operating system and the destination app you choose (e.g. WhatsApp, Messages).

Third Parties

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
